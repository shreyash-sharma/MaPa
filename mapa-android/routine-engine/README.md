# MaPa routine engine and rhythm model

The on-device brain of the parent app: a rule baseline, the five-state machine, a 10.5K-parameter rhythm model that learns each parent's day on their own phone, and the five guards that stop it learning an unwell week as normal. Everything runs on the parent's phone; only a state snapshot and alerts leave it.

All numbers below come from **synthetic** parents generated by `ml/mapa_rhythm/synth.py`. They show the engine works end to end and let rules and model be compared on equal footing. They are not evidence of real-world accuracy; the pilot is.

## Results (200 synthetic parents x 120 days, days 30 to 120)

A "positive" is a constructed 3 to 14-hour stretch where the phone is on but untouched. A false alert is an unusual-inactivity alert outside any positive or unwell stretch.

| Config | Recall | False alerts per parent-week | Median time to alert |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rules, 4 h awake silence | 0.80 | 2.63 | 3 h 30 m |
| **Model, budget 0.5/week** | 0.78 | **1.49** | 3 h 15 m |
| Rules, 5 h | 0.71 | 1.50 | 4 h 15 m |
| **Model, budget 0.25/week (default)** | 0.72 | **1.02** | 3 h 45 m |
| Rules, 6 h | 0.62 | 0.84 | 5 h 15 m |
| **Model, budget 0.05/week** | 0.63 | **0.63** | 5 h |

At matched recall the model sends **26% to 43% fewer false alerts** than the rules, and alerts as fast or faster. Times are from the start of the programmatically constructed silence. Full sweep, ablations and breakdowns: `results/harness_results.json`.

Other checks from the same run:

- **Stale data shown as fresh: 0 ticks** in every configuration. A family never saw Active or Quiet when the phone had not reported for over an hour.
- **Guards earn their keep.** With all guards off, the model at the same setting caught 0.52 of positives instead of 0.65; at equal false alerts the guarded model catches about 6 points more.
- **The 8-hour floor rule** adds 11 points of recall for 0.22 false alerts per parent-week. Kept on.
- **New routine question:** asked for 34 of 61 parents whose routine really shifted (the rest shifted too late in the run to see 3 weeks), median 26 days after the shift; 9 questions to parents whose routine had not changed.
- **Rhythm model vs personal profile alone** (validation parents): next-15-minute log-loss 0.373 vs 0.457, 18% lower; next-bin probabilities are well calibrated (predicted 0.38 vs observed 0.39 in the middle decile).
- **Int8 LiteRT** (`rhythm_step_int8.tflite`, 20 KB): mean next-bin probability error vs float 0.0018, log-loss unchanged.

### What still limits it

- **Most remaining false alerts are inherent to phone-only signals.** A parent who leaves the phone at home for 3 hours looks exactly like the start of a real problem. In the synthetic world that happens about once a week per parent; real rates are unknown until the pilot.
- **The weekly budget is not yet calibrated in absolute terms.** At a 0.25/week budget the model sends about 1 false alert per week, because flagged days (which hold the most surprising silences) are kept out of the calibrator. Family feedback on alerts ("this was fine") would fix this and is the next thing to build.
- **Light phone users** have ordinary 4 to 6 hour gaps. In a diagnostic run, the 12 parents with the most false alerts all used the phone in fewer than a quarter of their waking 15-minute slots.

## Design

| Piece | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Rule baseline | `RuleBaseline.kt` | Silence counted only over observed bins outside quiet hours (22:00 to 07:00). 4 h rule while learning, 8 h floor always. |
| Five states | `StateMachine.kt` | Paused, then monitoring unavailable, then active (30 min), then unusual, then quiet. Unusual latches until the next interaction. |
| Alert gate | `StateMachine.kt` | One alert per silence, 3 h cooldown, held through rest hours, one low-battery warning per discharge. |
| Freshness | `FamilyStatusResolver` | Server side: no sync for 60 min means monitoring unavailable, never an all-clear; one monitoring alert per outage after 2 h. |
| Rhythm model | `ml/mapa_rhythm/train.py`, `RhythmBackbone.kt` | GRU (48 hidden) over 16 derived features per 15 minutes; outputs the chance of first phone use in each of the next 16 bins. 10,544 parameters. Pretrained on 500 synthetic parents. |
| Personal model | `Profile.kt`, `Personal.kt` | Per-parent activity profile by day type, a 6-parameter personal head fitted on the phone in milliseconds, and a calibrator that turns the surprise of a silence into "how often does this happen for her". |
| Forecast | `RoutineEngine.forecast` | "Usually picks up the phone by 7:40": survival over the model's hazards, then the profile, up to 24 h ahead. |
| Training schedule | `TrainingScheduler` | Nightly through day 30, weekly after (Sunday night), only on charge, idle, 00:00 to 05:00. Rules decide days 1 to 14; the model decides from day 15 once it has 7 clean days. |
| Guards | `Guards.kt` | 7-day quarantine (fast lane while learning), life tags, anchored baseline with weekly step and drift limits, new-routine question after 3 weeks, weekly trend report worded as phone patterns. |
| Persistence | `RoutineEngine.snapshot/restore` | Versioned text snapshot of all state for Android's process restarts. |

What "trains on the phone" means here: the GRU core is the population model shipped with the app. The phone learns the personal profile, the personal head and the calibrator from its own parent's days, which is what moves the predictions. Fine-tuning the GRU itself on the phone is possible later through LiteRT's on-device training signatures; it is not needed to beat the rules.

One bug the harness caught: flagging a day as unusual whenever the plain rule fired meant light phone users had almost every day dropped by the quarantine guard, so their model never got ready and the rules kept deciding. Days are now flagged only by the floor rule or a ready model.

## Run it

```bash
# Kotlin engine and tests (32 tests, including Python parity and snapshot round-trip)
gradle :engine:test

# Python side (torch, litert-torch)
cd ml
python -m mapa_rhythm.train --out ../models            # about 10 minutes on 4 CPU cores
python -m mapa_rhythm.export --models ../models        # LiteRT float + int8, quantisation report
python -m mapa_rhythm.synth --parents 200 --days 120 --seed 3 --out ../data/harness

# Harness: every configuration on the same synthetic parents
gradle :harness:run --args="data/harness models/rhythm_v1.weights.txt results"
```
