# MaPa Rhythm Hybrid

**Code, apps and documentation:** [github.com/shreyash-sharma/MaPa](https://github.com/shreyash-sharma/MaPa) · **Dataset:** [MaPa-Rhythm](https://huggingface.co/datasets/shreyashsharma/MaPa-Rhythm) · Licence: Apache 2.0

## Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| What it is | A small causal model that learns one person's daily phone-use rhythm and scores how different the current day looks. |
| Size | 9,594 parameters, 21 inputs per 15-minute step |
| Runs on | Android, with ExecuTorch (XNNPACK). Exported with ExecuTorch 0.7.0; runs on the Android runtime 1.5.1. Two programs of 103,684 and 123,136 bytes. |
| Speed | 0.36 ms per 15-minute step on a Pixel 6a (median of 1,000 steps, instrumented test) |
| Held-out test | 39 people, opened once: caught all 93 injected disruptions (40/40 mild, 31/31 clear, 22/22 severe) with 2.4 false alerts per person-week. A 4-hour rule raised 6.1. |
| Training data | Trained on real smartphone logs, plus parent timelines generated by a statistical model learned from those logs. Every disruption is injected. |

**How to read the result.** On held-out people, MaPa's false alerts are exactly those of an 8-hour safety floor (2.4 per person-week);
every false alert of the deployed system comes from that floor. The model adds catches at no extra false alerts: on the test, 40 of 40
mild disruptions against 5 of 40 for the 8-hour rule alone.

This is a research model, not a medical, emergency or safety product.

## Limits

- Disruptions are injected, not observed emergencies. There is no evaluation with real parents in a real emergency.
- About 1% of the training days are real (532 of about 44,000 person-days); real 15-minute steps are 0.09% of training rows. The real people were mostly younger than the intended users.
- Normal days that only look quiet still trigger alerts too often. On the test, MaPa alerted inside 231 of 368 such episodes (62.8%); the 8-hour rule alerted in 139 of 368 (37.8%). (Definition: episodes whose expected answer is "no alert", counted as alerted if any alert falls inside the episode window; [evaluator code](https://github.com/shreyash-sharma/MaPa/blob/main/mapa-android/routine-engine/ml/mapa_train/decision.py).)
- The next-use forecast does not beat a simple personal profile on held-out people (test log-loss: model 0.5520, model-profile blend 0.4150, profile only 0.4125); the alert decision is what adds value.
- Day-one false alerts were measured on training people; day-one catch was shown on one staged day only (the benchmark has no injected disruptions in days 0 to 7).
- A training-only check without the safety floor had 0.000 false alerts per person-week. It was not selected and not tested on held-out people; do not read it as a held-out result.
- Inputs exclude movement, real battery and real charging in this version (the training data has placeholders for them).
- Built for families in India, but not validated on Indian older adults.
- An early pilot is planned; no accuracy claim is made from it.

## Uses

**Intended.** Inside the MaPa app, on the parent's own phone and with their consent: estimate when the phone is usually used, and flag a
day that looks very different from this person's own rhythm, so a family member can decide to call. Only the conclusion leaves the phone.

**Out of scope.** Medical use or diagnosis, emergency or fall detection, safety decisions, tracking anyone without consent, or decisions
about a person without a human in the loop. The model does not identify illness, sleep, mood or a person's physical state.

## How to get started

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python load_example.py     # checks the hashes, loads model.pt, runs one 15-minute step (19 outputs)
```

On Android, load `rhythm-step.pte` and `rhythm-daily.pte` with ExecuTorch. The app code, with a Kotlin fallback that gives the same numbers,
is in the GitHub repository (`ExecuTorchHybrid.kt`, `HybridPipeline.kt`).

## Architecture

- **Today:** a causal dilated 1D CNN followed by a GRU, one step per 15 minutes.
- **Recent days:** an LSTM over up to 30 daily summaries.
- **This person:** a personal context from the person's own history, with a starting profile when history is short.
- **Outputs (19):** the chance of phone use in each of the next 16 steps, use now, an unusual-quiet score and a confidence score.
- **Decision:** a combined score against an alert line learned from the person's own normal days, raised when confidence is low, with an
  8-hour (32-step) safety floor. Old data is always reported as "monitoring unavailable" by the app, never as a normal day.

The first next-use probability is blended as 0.15 × model + 0.85 × the person's profile; the weight was chosen on training people only.

## Training data and method

Data: [MaPa-Rhythm](https://huggingface.co/datasets/shreyashsharma/MaPa-Rhythm) `final-v2`. Split by person: train 162 people
(4,230,516 rows), validation 39 (495,523), test 39 (492,687). Train has 1,624 injected behaviour episodes (781 mild, 502 clear,
341 severe). 11 more real people are held out of all fitting. Real rows come from S3 and UbiqLog
([attribution](DATA-ATTRIBUTION.md), [S3 licence](licenses/s3-CC-BY-4.0.txt), [UbiqLog licence](licenses/ubiqlog-CC-BY-4.0.txt)).

Three stages: 3 epochs of next-use pretraining on real observed steps (3,828 steps from 22 people), 5 epochs on derived normal
timelines, and 7 epochs on injected behaviour episodes. Day-swap augmentation (five nearest same-person days within the 10th-percentile
distance), source weights, and random history lengths from 0 to 30 days so the model works with little history. The configuration was
chosen by five-fold cross-validation over training people, then the model was fitted on all training people.

## Export and parity

ExecuTorch 0.7.0 / XNNPACK exports: 103,684 bytes (step) and 123,136 bytes (daily). ExecuTorch against PyTorch: max error 2.384e-6
(192 streaming steps) and 1.788e-7 (daily, 0 to 30 days of history). Kotlin against PyTorch: 9.537e-7 and 8.941e-8. On the phone, the
full pipeline gives the same alerts as the tested Python pipeline on 40 days (960 steps compared, max logit error 1.4e-6). The MaPa parent
app runs this model on ExecuTorch (Android runtime 1.5.1).

## Prior work we learned from

Ideas adopted, no code or data reused. None of these is the paper of this model; MaPa has no paper yet.

- Shen et al. (2025), arXiv preprint, id 2509.23158 (presented at IEEE BHI 2025): day-swap augmentation, similarity weighting, a daily-summary sequence model.
- Gibietz et al. (2026), Gerontechnology: learn normal patterns per person and adapt the alert line from recent normal days.
- Cejudo et al. (2025): predict the expected routine and score deviations from it.
- The apps ElderWatch AI and eNeighbor (alert levels, morning check) and StillFine (ask the parent first) informed product experiments.
