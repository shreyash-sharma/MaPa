# MaPa-Rhythm

Derived timelines are generated by models fitted to real phone logs. Every disruption is injected.

This dataset contains observed strict-heartbeat bins from S3 and UbiqLog gen_fit people, model-derived parents, and injected disruptions with separate injected/expected labels. Logger heartbeat implies observation; it does not verify foreground permission or genuine non-use. Derived parents: 200 × 120 days, seed 20261101. The censored-KM generator was fitted only on release training people. The 11 real_check people are excluded from fitting and release.

| Daily count proxy mean / SD / median | 29.160 / 21.494 / 24.0 | 64.451 / 35.817 / 59.0 |
| Exact real-day copies | 0 | 0 |

The quiet-length comparison is worse than synth_hand and is retained. Fidelity uses identical strict masks and censoring on 118 matched day pairs from nine test people, predominantly younger than the age-calibration cohort. Historical diagnostic test exposure predates this release split; this is not pristine confirmatory evidence. Full numbers, masks, counts and caveats are in fidelity.json. AUC below 0.5 must not be interpreted as automatic indistinguishability.

## Calibration and assumptions

Only the published unlock/day mean 28.7 and SD 21.4 from Shen et al., Innovation in Aging 2026;10(4):igag007, Table 1, DOI 10.1093/geroni/igag007, as cited in routine-engine/CALIBRATION.md, calibrate the age layer. Foreground counts are a session/unlock proxy, not identical measurements. No screen-time target is claimed. Inherited night blocks and extrapolation beyond censored observations are assumptions. age_calibration.json and sampled_parameters.json give every applied scaling value and its citation. Small US older-adult aggregates do not establish applicability to Indian parents or Android.

## Episodes and evaluation limits

Ten scenarios are injected into eligible strict real windows and derived timelines. Normal quiet is selected without changing counts. Routine shifts span seven days; life-tag days are excluded from model training. Missing bins are represented as explicit clock-grid tombstones with present=false and interactions=-1. Both labels remain on each episode row. The episode manifest reports real counts separately; unsupported real cells remain zero. Routine confirmation is not treated as a binary emergency catch. Test person groups and scenario definitions were frozen before release training. Untouched-day false alerts and injected catch rates are separate. All failures and trials are retained.

No medical or safety claims. An injected disruption is not an observed emergency. No location, WiFi, Bluetooth, voice or raw app-name payloads are included. Licence text and attribution are saved per source. No eval-only source is redistributed.

## Loading

Run `python final-v2/loader.py` to reload and verify the three Parquet splits. With Hugging Face Datasets, use `load_dataset("parquet", data_files={s: "final-v2/"+s+".parquet" for s in ["train","val","test"]})`. Filter `subset` for observed, derived or episodes. Splits are by parent/person; episode variants stay with their parent. Never treat separate episodes from one parent as independent people. final/ is immutable; revisions go to final-v2/.

## Field-level assumptions

For the observed subset, real_observed identifies the real interaction-count stream and heartbeat timing, not independently measured hardware telemetry. Healthy phone/permission/network flags are harness assumptions based on strict logger coverage. Charging follows the harness night mask and battery defaults to 80; these are control placeholders, not source battery/charging measurements. Injected monitoring faults alter those controls explicitly. Do not use these fields as evidence of real device health, location, or personal safety.

Streaming state resets at day boundaries to match training and evaluation. The default Hugging Face configuration selects the current frozen version; observed, derived and episodes are separate configurations, each retaining the same person splits.

## Detector results on fixed test people

Model training and detector evaluation are deferred at the user’s request. This deliverable is the dataset only; no detector-performance gate is claimed as passing.

## Known paired-label limitation

The count/observation inputs are unchanged from the untouched parent in 118 of 425 original-v1 training positives and 28 of 83 validation positives. Applying a zeroing transform can be a no-op on already quiet windows. These cases remain disclosed in paired-input-audit.json; no frozen records or test labels were removed to improve scores. Injected stationary movement is an assumption and is not an input to the released behaviour classifier.

## Dataset release status

The active frozen version is final-v2/. It contains 4,230,516 training rows, 495,523 validation rows and 492,687 test rows. The original final/ is retained as an immutable earlier version. Validation and test payloads are byte-identical across the two versions. Model training is deferred; C4 detector comparisons are not a dataset-release acceptance claim. Known fidelity failures, censored-tail assumptions, historical holdout exposure and paired-label limitations remain disclosed above. See DATASET_CHECKS.json for the completed data audit.
